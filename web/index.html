FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a 1-hour video take?

Usually a fraction of the video's running time; transcription is the longest step. You can close the tab, because your project keeps processing and the clips are waiting when you're back.

Can I paste a YouTube link instead of uploading?

Yes. Paste a link from YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, Twitch, Dropbox or any direct video URL, up to 3 hours long. Only use videos you own or have permission to repurpose. Private videos need to be made public or unlisted first, or uploaded as a file.

Can I change what the AI picked?

Yes. Edit the hook text, change the caption style, colour or layout, or nudge the start and end by a second at a time, then re-render. You can also tell the AI what to look for before you upload ("only the bits about mortgages").

What about videos with two people or a screen share?

Speaker tracking follows the main face and cuts cleanly when the shot changes. When there's no face, like slides or a screen recording, the clip is fitted over a blurred copy of itself so nothing important is cropped away.

Which languages work?

Transcription supports 90+ languages and the AI writes hooks and captions in the language of the video.

Do you keep my videos?

Your uploads are used only to make your clips. Delete a project and its source video, transcript and clips are removed.
